I'm Ferdinand Fojtlin, an independent BI consultant based in Bratislava. I work with Power BI, data pipelines and automation — helping companies and individuals turn messy spreadsheets and exports into dashboards people actually trust and act on.

## How I work

I don't like guessing where I can measure instead. Before I build a report, I want to understand where the data comes from, how often it changes, and where it could be misleading. Only then does the visual design matter.

That's exactly how the **[VIE/BTS Aviation Tracker](/projekty/vie-bts-tracker/)** came about — a project I built on my own to systematically track how Vienna and Bratislava airports trade passengers between them. The full code and methodology are public — the best way to show how I actually work with data is to let you look under the hood.

## What I enjoy

- Building data pipelines that run themselves and don't need manual upkeep
- Dashboards someone actually opens again a week after delivery — not just on presentation day
- Explaining data to people who aren't analysts, without dumbing it down
